## 7. Post-production notes

- **Music:** a soft, warm acoustic or ambient bed (light piano, oud harmonics or gentle guitar) at around 80–90 BPM, licensed for social use. Keep it subtle and add light natural sound: spoon on glass, pouring, a cup set down.
- **Cuts:** cut on action, such as the spoon stir, the cup lift and the glass placed on the counter. Use no transitions other than straight cuts and one soft dissolve into Shot 7.
- **Grade:** match all clips to one warm-neutral LUT so that skin tones stay consistent across generations.
- **Packaging:** the container is shown unbranded. If this is for a specific brand, replace it with the real product in Shot 7 (composite or image-to-video) and get the brand's approval for the final frame.
- **Compliance:** show no claims on screen and no before-and-after material. Any caption copy should describe the routine ("my morning ritual"), not results.
- **Disclosure:** the people are AI-generated, so follow each platform's AI-content labelling rules (Meta, TikTok and YouTube all require a label for realistic synthetic people).
