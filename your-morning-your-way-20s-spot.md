# "Your Morning, Your Way": 20-Second Collagen Spot

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 · 4K · 24 fps · 20 seconds · no dialogue, VO, subtitles or invented logos
**Idea:** one woman, one morning, three ways to take collagen. The film is about her choice, not about results.
**Rhythm:** five 4-second movements joined by **match cuts**: each beat hands its motion (a swirl, a lift, a pour) to the next, so the 20 seconds play as one continuous flowing gesture.

> **Generation tip:** most tools produce clips of 5 seconds or more. Generate each shot at 5–6 s and trim to the timings below. Create one still reference portrait of the woman first and use it as the image or character reference for every shot.

## 2. Storyboard and shot prompts

### Beat 1: Water · 0:00–0:04
**1A (0:00–0:02), macro:** a brushed-gold scoop tips fine white collagen powder into a crystal-clear glass of still water on the travertine counter. The powder falls in a soft cascade through a shaft of golden sunlight and blooms gently in the water. Backlit, with sharp particle detail and a slow push-in.
**1B (0:02–0:04), close-up:** Layla's hand stirs with a slim gold spoon. The water swirls and clears with a delicate shimmer, and her face is softly out of focus behind the glass, catching the morning light.
**→ Transition:** the clockwise swirl in the water **match-cuts** to the clockwise swirl in the coffee.

### Beat 2: Coffee · 0:04–0:08
**2A (0:04–0:06), top-down macro:** collagen is stirred into a handmade cream ceramic cup of freshly brewed black coffee. The spoon's swirl matches the previous shot, and steam curls up through the sunlight.
**2B (0:06–0:08), medium close-up:** Layla lifts the cup with both hands, closes her eyes for a moment, and breathes in the aroma with a quiet, private calm. Slow arc from her three-quarter profile, with the window glowing behind her.
**→ Transition:** the upward lift of the cup **cuts on motion** to the downward pour of the juice.

### Beat 3: Orange juice · 0:08–0:12
**3A (0:08–0:10), macro, high-speed look:** freshly squeezed orange juice pours into a short fluted glass. The rich citrus colour glows against the cream counter, pulp and tiny bubbles catch the light, and halved oranges sit on an oak board in the soft background.
**3B (0:10–0:12), macro:** a scoop of collagen goes into the juice and a spoon stirs it in, blending naturally, with realistic liquid movement and condensation on the glass.
**→ Transition:** a slow pull-back from the juice glass widens into the counter tableau.

### Beat 4: The choice · 0:12–0:16
**4A (0:12–0:14), elegant tableau:** the glass of water, the coffee cup and the orange juice stand in a clean row on the travertine counter beside the collagen container, with golden light raking across all three. A slow lateral slider move.
**4B (0:14–0:16), close-up on hands:** Layla's hand enters the frame and hovers for a beat, then naturally chooses the coffee cup. A small, human, unhurried gesture.
**→ Transition:** the cup lifts out of frame and the camera settles on the hero composition.

### Beat 5: Hero · 0:16–0:20
**5 (0:16–0:20), hero shot:** in the foreground and in sharp focus, the matte cream collagen container stands beside the coffee cup with steam rising, in warm sunlight. In the soft-focus background, Layla leans against the counter, holding nothing, and gives a soft, genuine smile towards the window, not at the camera. A very slow push-in that settles to a still frame. **Keep the upper third of the frame clean and uncluttered** for the brand logo and tagline.
