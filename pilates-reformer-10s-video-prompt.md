# Reformer Flow — 10-Second AI Video Prompt Pack

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 · 4K · 24 fps · exactly 10 seconds · no dialogue, VO, text, logos or watermarks
**Use:** Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
**Built for:** Veo, Sora, Kling, Runway, Hailuo and similar tools. Use the single-prompt version (section 5) for tools that make a full 10 s clip in one pass. For the cleanest anatomy and Reformer mechanics, generate the three shots separately (section 4) and cut them together.

### Reformer mechanics (include in every shot with movement)

> She lies on her back on the carriage, with her head on the headrest and both shoulders against the shoulder rests. Her arms rest long by her sides with her palms down on the carriage. Her feet are on the footbar, and the springs are attached. As she straightens her legs, the carriage glides smoothly along the rails **away from the footbar** and the springs stretch with visible resistance. Her feet stay on the footbar the whole time. As she bends her knees, the carriage glides back **towards the footbar** under control and the springs shorten. Her pelvis stays level and her spine stays neutral on the carriage. Her knees track over her toes and never lock or hyperextend. The movement is slow, even and continuous.

## 4. Shot list and prompts (total 10 s)

| # | Time | Duration | Shot | Purpose |
|---|------|----------|------|---------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | 3 s | Set-up (hook) | Stop the scroll and establish the woman, the machine and the space |
| 2 | 0:03–0:07 | 4 s | Press and return | One clean, complete repetition |
| 3 | 0:07–0:10 | 3 s | Focus and finish | Emotion, control and a confident final frame |

**Shot 1: Set-up and hook (0:00–0:03)**
Open on a low, slow tracking shot that glides along the oak rail of the Reformer from the foot end, with the brushed-steel footbar sharp in the foreground catching the daylight. Within the first second, rack focus to Maya lying on the carriage at medium distance. She places the balls of both feet onto the footbar, heels lifted and legs together, then settles her shoulders against the shoulder rests and takes a slow breath in. The camera rises slightly as it moves, with her reflection soft in the mirror behind.

**Shot 2: Press and return (0:03–0:07)**
A side-on medium-wide shot at carriage height, with the camera tracking very slowly alongside the machine so that her whole body and the full rail are in frame. Maya exhales and straightens her legs smoothly, pushing the carriage away from the footbar until her legs are long but not locked. The springs visibly stretch. She pauses for a beat at full extension, then inhales as she bends her knees and lets the carriage glide back towards the footbar with steady control. Her upper body stays still and relaxed, her arms long by her sides, and the movement comes only from the legs.

**Shot 3: Focus and finish (0:07–0:10)**
- *3a, close-up (0:07–0:08.5):* Her face and shoulders in soft profile against the cream headrest, with a gentle push-in. Her gaze is calm and focused, and she breathes out slowly through slightly parted lips. A hint of the moving carriage and the springs is soft in the background.
- *3b, final frame (0:08.5–0:10):* A clean high angle from above the foot end. She presses out one last time and holds still at full extension, heels together and toes apart in Pilates stance on the footbar, her body long and aligned on the carriage. She allows a small, confident half-smile. The camera settles and ends on a still, elegant frame.

## 7. Post-production notes

- **Hook:** the first two seconds rely on the footbar foreground and the rack focus to Maya. If a generation opens slowly, trim the first 6–12 frames rather than regenerating.
- **Cuts:** cut on action at the start of the leg press (Shot 1 → 2) and at the exhale (Shot 2 → 3a). Use straight cuts only and keep the final frame still for at least 1 second so it works as a loop point.
- **Duration check:** export at exactly 10:00 seconds (240 frames at 24 fps). If the platform loops, make sure the last frame flows naturally back into the first.
- **Sound:** a minimal, modern ambient or downtempo bed at around 85–95 BPM, licensed for social use, with subtle natural sound: the carriage gliding on the rails, a soft spring stretch and a quiet exhale.
- **Grade:** match all clips to one warm-neutral LUT so that skin tones, the beige outfit and the oak frame stay consistent across generations.
- **Quality check before delivery:** check frame by frame that the feet stay on the bar, there are five toes and fingers, the carriage direction matches the leg movement, the springs are present in every shot, and the outfit and hair do not change.
- **Branding:** the clip is deliberately unbranded. Any logo, product or end card should be added in the edit with the brand's approval.
- **Disclosure:** the woman is AI-generated, so follow each platform's AI-content labelling rules (Meta, TikTok and YouTube all require a label for realistic synthetic people).
